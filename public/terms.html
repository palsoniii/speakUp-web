What SpeakUp is

SpeakUp is a daily spoken-fluency practice tool. You record short spoken answers to prompts, and get feedback that's partly counted directly from your transcript (pace, filler words, pauses) and partly generated by an AI model reading what you said. It's a personal practice tool, not a substitute for professional speech, language, or communication coaching.
