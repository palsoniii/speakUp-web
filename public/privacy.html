Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026 · Applies to: SpeakUp (the web app at speakup-daily.vercel.app)

What we collect

DataWhy
Email + passwordYour account, via Supabase Auth. We never see your password in plain text — Supabase handles authentication.
Name, age (both optional)Personalizing the app (greeting you by name). Age isn't currently used for anything else, but is stored if you enter it.
Audio recordingsYour practice sessions, so you can play them back and track progress over time.
Transcripts of your recordingsGenerated from your audio (see "Third parties" below) to power pace/filler-word feedback and AI coaching.
Session metadataDate, duration, self-rating, optional written notes, and which exercise you did — your practice history.
AI-generated feedbackStored alongside each session so you can look back at it later.

We don't collect payment information (SpeakUp is free), location data, or contacts. We don't run analytics or advertising trackers.

Third parties who process your data

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage. Your account data, sessions, transcripts, and audio recordings live on Supabase's infrastructure.
  • Groq — an AI inference provider. When you finish a recording, your audio (for transcription) and/or transcript (for coaching feedback) is sent to Groq's API to generate the feedback you see, labeled "AI read" in the app. Groq processes this per its own terms and privacy policy; we don't control how long Groq itself retains request data on its end.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else beyond what's needed to run the service as described above.

Where your recordings live

Recordings are stored in cloud storage reachable by a private, unguessable link — not listed or searchable anywhere — but technically not access-controlled the way your account data is. Don't share a recording's direct link with anyone you wouldn't want to have it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Delete your account (see below) and we'll delete your sessions, recordings, and profile data from our systems — this doesn't retroactively delete anything already processed by Groq as part of generating past feedback.

Your choices

  • Access/export: your session history is visible in-app at any time (Progress → History).
  • Delete your account: currently a manual process — email palsonirocks@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and associated data.
  • Turn off AI features: live captions can be turned off in Settings. Core AI coaching feedback currently runs automatically for every session with a transcript.

Children's privacy

SpeakUp does not currently verify age at signup. SpeakUp is intended for users aged 13 and up. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at palsonirocks@gmail.com and we'll remove it.

Security

We use industry-standard practices where we can — encrypted connections, access-controlled database rows (so your account only ever sees your own sessions), and API keys kept server-side, never in the app you download. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee against every possible breach.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or concerns about your data: palsonirocks@gmail.com.
